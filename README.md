# Poopify 💩 https://poopify-0.web.app/
### *The "Quantified Self" app you never knew you needed.*

Poopify turns your daily "output" into a gamified experience. From tracking streaks to generating your "Annual Poopy Wrapped," Poopify combines health insights with the hilarity of social competition.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Daily Cards:** Log your session and get classified (Type 1-7 on the Bristol Scale). Get personality-driven cards like "The Philosopher" or "The Sniper."
* **The Streak (Duolingo Style):** Don't break the chain! Receive reminders that actually care about your digestive consistency.
* **Poopy Wrapped:** A year-end summary of your "transit stats," most common locations, and total time "invested."
* **Global/Regional Leaderboards:** Compete anonymously. *“A user in Kerala has logged 3x more than you today!”*
* **Insights:** Personalized data trends that correlate your habits with your health.
* **Heat Map:** A visual "contribution map" to see your footprint across the globe.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript (React Native/Flutter)
* **Backend:** Python / Java
* **Data Visualization:** Power BI / Excel logic for insights
* **Database:** SQL for structured log history

Your data is private. Poopify ensures that while your stats are competitive, your identity and specific logs stay secure and encrypted.
